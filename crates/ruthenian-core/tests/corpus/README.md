# Conformance corpus

`paradigms.tsv` — every cell of every paradigm `docs/RUTHENIAN.md` states, as a
committed artifact. Generated by `tools/extract_paradigms.py`; **do not
hand-edit.**

```
pos     lemma   features            form     section
noun    dom     Genitive.Singular   domogo   3.3
```

The `section` column is the specification section the cell came from, so a
failure names where to look rather than only what broke.

## What is in it

264 cells: 11 nominal paradigms × 24 (8 cases × 3 numbers).

| Lemma | § | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| `dom` | 3.3 | declension II masculine, hard — the reference paradigm |
| `Konj` | 3.3 | the same, soft. Capitalised because §3.3 tabulates it with the animate accusative `konja` |
| `Drug` | 3.3 | velar stem: the vocative `druzzje` and locative `druzi` differ in **both** the consonant and the vowel, which is the second palatalization earning its place |
| `okno`, `polje` | 3.4 | declension II neuter, hard and soft |
| `zzena` | 3.5 | declension I, hard |
| `kniga` | 3.5 | velar feminine: genitive `knigi` against dative `knizi`, distinguished by the palatalization alone |
| `zjemlja` | 3.5 | declension I, soft |
| `nacija` | 3.5 | the **vowel-final** stem `naci-`, taking `zjemlja`'s endings unchanged; `ija`, `ijy` and `iji` are the sequences a contraction rule would silently eat |
| `sluga'` | 3.5 | masculine in agreement, declension I in form — and its accusative is `slugu`, *not* the ablative |
| `noczj'` | 3.6 | declension III, and the mark that separates it from a soft masculine II |

The specification's `= nom` and `= dat` shorthand is expanded, so the engine is
asked all 24 questions rather than only the 15 with distinct answers. Syncretism
then shows up as repeated forms — `doma` is both the ablative singular and the
nominative dual, inherited from OCS and not a defect.

## The two directions of drift

- **The specification cannot move away from the corpus.** The header carries an
  FNV-1a checksum of `docs/RUTHENIAN.md`, and the `spec_currency` guard
  recomputes it. Any spec edit fails the suite until the corpus is regenerated
  and its diff reviewed.
- **The corpus cannot claim a form the specification does not contain.** The
  script verifies every form against the backticked tokens of the spec before
  writing. A transcription typo fails loudly.

## Regenerating

```bash
python3 tools/extract_paradigms.py
```

It prints the cell count and the new checksum. Commit the corpus together with
the spec change that caused it.
